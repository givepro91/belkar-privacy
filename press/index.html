About

Yoon Siwoo was a porter, left to die in a sealed rift by his own raid party. At the edge of death he makes a pact with Belkar, an exiled demon: "Feed me your enemies. I will give you their power."

Belkar is a landscape pixel action roguelite built for touch. Attacks are automatic; the player moves and decides when to use three buttons: dodge, weapon skill, and the pact strike that devours a boss's power. The story follows the hunt from the three traitors of the raid party, through awakened criminals and the lords of hell, to the Gate of Heaven. Three endings, then two more parts after the credits.

Features

  • No boss is met the same way twice. Each target takes three stages. Before the showdown, every boss shows up its own way (duel, chase, command, ambush, siege or harass) and retreats. Drive it off fast and its weakness grows in the final fight.
  • Steal what you kill. 4 weapons and 18 boss powers to devour, equip and build around.
  • Erosion. Every death lets the darkness climb the hero's body. His look and the ending change with it.
  • A hideout that grows. Companions, a trophy hall of statues of the bosses you beat, rematches in the Rift of Memory, a daily bounty and 33 achievements (Game Center).
  • Three parts of story. A 7-act main story, then Part 2 and Part 3 after the ending.
