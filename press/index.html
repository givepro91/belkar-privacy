About

Yoon Siwoo was a porter, left to die in a sealed rift by his own raid party. At the edge of death he makes a pact with Belkar, an exiled demon: "Feed me your enemies. I will give you their power."

Belkar is a top-down pixel action roguelite, played in landscape and built for touch. Attacks are automatic; the player moves and decides when to use three buttons: dodge, weapon skill, and the pact (the ultimate) that devours a boss's power. The story follows the hunt from the three traitors of the raid party, through awakened criminals and the lords of hell, to the Gate of Heaven. Three endings, then two more parts after the credits.

Features

  • No boss is met the same way twice. Traitors, sinners and dukes take three stages; lords take two. Before the showdown, every boss shows up its own way (duel, chase, command, ambush, siege or harass) and retreats. Drive it off fast and its weakness grows in the final fight.
  • A different build every run. 4 weapons, abilities like Blood Blades, Dark Meteor and Crow Swarm that evolve, and 18 boss powers to devour and equip. Swift, iron, splitting and volatile elites join as the waves rise.
  • Erosion. Every death, Belkar rewinds the hero's heart, and the cost builds up as Erosion. Black cracks climb his body, and the ending changes with it.
  • Gentle at first. The first targets carry fewer rift mutators, and repeated deaths on the same stage earn Belkar's Blessing (less damage taken) until you break through.
  • A hideout that grows. Companions, a trophy hall of statues of the bosses you beat, rematches in the Rift of Memory, a daily bounty and 33 achievements (Game Center).
  • Three parts of story. A 7-act main story, then Part 2 and Part 3 after the ending.
